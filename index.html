Before you visit

Do I need a reservation?

Booking ahead keeps your preferred time, and there’s no deposit to reserve. Text or call 0995 574 2688.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. We’re open daily from 2 PM to 1 AM. Call before you come and we’ll tell you the wait. (Example)

What time is last call?

Last call is 12:00 AM. Every treatment finishes by 1:00 AM, when we close, so from 11:30 PM we book 1-hour massages only.

Can I choose my therapist?

Yes. Request your therapist by name when you book. If you don’t, we’ll assign one from that day’s schedule.

Can I come with friends or family?

Yes. Each room is used by one group at a time, so we give your group a room that fits and never share it with another group.

Can I request a specific pressure?

Of course. Tell your therapist light, medium or firm before you start, or at any point during your treatment. (Example)

What’s the difference between dry and oil massage?

Swedish is oil-based, for relaxation and stress relief. Shiatsu and Foot & Back are dry; Shiatsu works pressure points and deep tissue.

When can I book a foot spa?

The 45-minute Foot Spa is available before 6 PM only.

Is anything included with my treatment?

Every visit includes complimentary warm tea. (Example)

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, GCash and major credit cards. (Example)

Is parking available?

Rosemont Tower has parking for guests on Panay Ave. (Example)

Do you have other branches?

Yes. Besides Quezon City, we’re in BGC and Banawe, all open daily from 2 PM to 1 AM. Prices may differ by branch.
